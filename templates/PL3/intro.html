Multi-Product Inventory Optimization

The Multi-Product Inventory Optimization Problem involves determining optimal ordering and storage strategies for multiple products over multiple time periods, considering various constraints such as storage capacity, product expiration, and setup costs.

Real-World Applications

1. Retail and Distribution

  • Supermarket Chains: Managing perishable goods inventory across multiple stores.

2. Pharmaceutical Industry

  • Medical Supplies: Balancing inventory of various medical products with limited shelf life.

3. Food and Beverage Industry

  • Food Distribution: Optimizing perishable food storage and ordering.

4. Manufacturing

  • Raw Materials: Managing multiple components with different storage requirements.

Mathematical Formulation

1. Sets and Indices

  • Time periods: \( T = \{1, \ldots, |T|\} \)
  • Products: \( P = \{1, \ldots, |P|\} \)

2. Parameters

  • Demand: \( d_{p,t} \) for product \(p\) in period \(t\)
  • Holding cost: \( h_p \) for product \(p\)
  • Ordering cost: \( c_p \) per unit of product \(p\)
  • Setup cost: \( s_p \) for ordering product \(p\)
  • Capacity: \( Cap_p \) maximum storage for product \(p\)
  • Shared capacity: \( SCap \) total warehouse capacity
  • Expiration periods: \( e_p \) shelf life of product \(p\)

3. Decision Variables

\[ x_{p,t} = \text{order quantity of product } p \text{ in period } t \] \[ I_{p,t} = \text{inventory level of product } p \text{ at end of period } t \] \[ y_{p,t} = \begin{cases} 1 & \text{if an order is placed for product } p \text{ in period } t \\ 0 & \text{otherwise} \end{cases} \]

4. Objective Function

Minimize total costs:

\[ \text{Minimize} \sum_{p \in P} \sum_{t \in T} (c_p x_{p,t} + s_p y_{p,t} + h_p I_{p,t}) \]

5. Constraints

Inventory Balance:

\[ I_{p,t} = I_{p,t-1} + x_{p,t} - d_{p,t} \quad \forall p \in P, t \in T \]

Initial Inventory:

\[ I_{p,0} = x_{p,0} - d_{p,0} \quad \forall p \in P \]

Expiration:

\[ I_{p,t} \leq \sum_{k=\max(0,t-e_p+1)}^t x_{p,k} \quad \forall p \in P, t \in T \]

Individual Capacity:

\[ I_{p,t} \leq Cap_p \quad \forall p \in P, t \in T \]

Shared Capacity:

\[ \sum_{p \in P} I_{p,t} \leq SCap \quad \forall t \in T \]

Setup Forcing:

\[ x_{p,t} \leq M_p y_{p,t} \quad \forall p \in P, t \in T \] where \( M_p = \sum_{t \in T} d_{p,t} \)

Non-negativity:

\[ I_{p,t}, x_{p,t} \geq 0 \quad \forall p \in P, t \in T \]

Implementation Notes

This formulation is implemented using the Gurobi optimization solver, which:

  • Creates continuous variables for order quantities (\(x_{p,t}\)) and inventory levels (\(I_{p,t}\))
  • Uses binary variables (\(y_{p,t}\)) for setup decisions
  • Implements all capacity and expiration constraints
  • Handles both individual and shared storage limitations
  • Considers setup costs and minimum order quantities
